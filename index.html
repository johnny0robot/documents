Johnny Classroom

To join a class, if you want to join with the class code, type the class code on the teacher’s screen, and click “Join Class”. Then it will pop-up on your screen. If you want to join by invite, open Johnny Classroom, and you will see a notfiction at the top of your screen saying “You have been invited to join” + class + button “Deny” and + button “Accept”. Click either. whatever you click, every teacher owning the class will get a notification saying if you accepted the invition or denyed it. If you denyed it, they have the option to resend the invasion. There is anthor way to accept or deny the email. Open JMail, click on the email with the subject that sounds something like someone + “has sent you an invite to join the class” + class + button “Accept Invite” + button “Deny Invite”. Click either. Same thing, the sender will get a notification about if you denyed or accepted it. They can still resend it.
